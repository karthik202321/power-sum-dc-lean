MATHLIB v4.19.0 INFRASTRUCTURE FOR THE ORIGINAL CONORMAL PROOF

This inventory records a search of the pinned local Mathlib source. It is
an implementation map, not a proof of the paper's geometric statements.
Availability below means actual source definitions/theorems were located.
Missing means no ready theorem or construction was found in this version;
it does not mean that the mathematics cannot be formalized.

INDEPENDENT GENERIC INCIDENCE CHAIN

GenericKernelLifts.lean contains the actual ordinary/symmetric kernel-line
and finite-family lift constructions formerly in ExactKernelLifts.lean.
The namespace Sheshadri.ExactKernelLifts is retained for compatibility.
ExactKernelLifts.lean now contains only exists_fermat_ordinary_lift_family
and exists_fermat_symmetric_lift_family and imports the generic module
together with ExplicitProjectiveSlice.

GeneralIncidenceCount imports GenericKernelLifts. Transitive source-import
inspection shows no Explicit* or Expanded* dependencies in
GenericKernelLifts, GeneralIncidenceCount, IncidenceSimple, IncidenceBezout,
PolynomialFamilies, or ZariskiBridge. This separates generic ingredients
from their former concrete-slice applications; it does not yet identify
the generic root data with slices of an actual conormal cycle.

The three changed/new targets and the full existing default lake build
succeeded after this refactor. No theorem statement was weakened. The
only replacement dependency needed was Mathlib.Algebra.CharZero.Infinite,
for the Infinite C instance formerly obtained through the explicit slice.

SCHEMES, FIBRES, AND CLOSURES: AVAILABLE BUILDING BLOCKS

* AlgebraicGeometry/Scheme, Spec, AffineScheme, Limits, Pullbacks, Fiber,
  Stalk, and ResidueField provide actual schemes, morphisms, fibre products,
  stalks, and residue fields.
* AlgebraicGeometry/Properties and Noetherian provide reduced/integral
  schemes and Noetherian facts. finite_irreducibleComponents_of_isNoetherian
  gives finitely many topological irreducible components.
* AlgebraicGeometry/FunctionField defines Scheme.functionField at the
  generic point and proves it is a field for integral schemes, with the
  expected injectivity statements.
* AlgebraicGeometry/IdealSheaf defines Scheme.IdealSheafData, its support,
  radical, and morphism kernel ideal sheaf. Scheme.Hom.support_ker identifies
  its support with the closure of the image under quasi-compactness.
  There is affine quotient/gluing data for ideal-sheaf subschemes.
* AlgebraicGeometry/AffineScheme defines specTargetImageIdeal,
  specTargetImage, affineTargetImage, and actual image factorizations for
  affine targets. These are useful for local schematic closure calculations.
* AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/ClosedImmersion has actual closed immersions,
  quotient-ring examples, composition, and base-change stability.
* RingTheory/Localization/Ideal provides extension/contraction identities,
  including IsLocalization.map_comap and the prime/disjointness version of
  contraction after extension. This supports local closure-by-contraction
  constructions, but no completed conormal-family closure construction is
  supplied by those identities alone.

FLATNESS AND DVRs

* RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/Basic defines IsDiscreteValuationRing,
  extending IsPrincipalIdealRing and IsLocalRing, and supplies uniformizer
  and ideal structure results. Valuation and fraction/localization theory
  are available in their own modules.
* RingTheory/Flat/Basic defines Module.Flat via tensor exactness/injectivity
  criteria. Module.Flat.of_free, localization and base-change results are
  available. Flat/EquationalCriterion provides the relation criterion.
* AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Flat defines actual flat scheme morphisms.
  AlgebraicGeometry.Flat.iff_flat_stalkMap and Flat.of_stalkMap give the
  local-ring criterion; composition and base change are proved.
* LinearAlgebra/FreeModule/PID proves
  Module.free_of_finite_type_torsion_free' and
  Module.free_iff_noZeroSMulDivisors for FINITE modules over a PID.
  Together with Module.Flat.of_free, this supplies flatness for finite
  torsion-free modules over a DVR.
* No general arbitrary-module torsion-free-implies-flat theorem over a
  valuation ring/PID/DVR was found. Coordinate rings of finite-type
  families are generally not finite modules over the base DVR, so the
  finite-module theorem must not be applied merely from finite TYPE.
  A proof using the equational criterion, or directed unions of finite
  free submodules, remains needed for that general route.
* RingTheory/Spectrum/Prime/FreeLocus proves isOpen_freeLocus for finitely
  presented modules and isLocallyConstant_rankAtStalk for finitely
  presented flat modules. This is a finite-module rank theorem, not
  constancy of projective fibre multidegrees or a generic-flatness theorem
  for arbitrary finite-type families.

PROJECTIVITY AND PROPERNESS

* AlgebraicGeometry/ProjectiveSpectrum constructs Proj of a graded algebra,
  its scheme, basic affine charts, stalks, and the structure map to Spec of
  degree zero. It also has the projective-spectrum Zariski topology.
* IMPORTANT: ProjectiveSpectrum/Proper.lean currently proves separatedness
  of Proj. Its documentation explicitly leaves the valuative criterion as
  a TODO. The filename is not evidence that general Proj properness is
  already proved.
* AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Proper defines IsProper (separated,
  universally closed, locally of finite type), proves composition/base
  change, and proves finite morphisms proper.
* AlgebraicGeometry/ValuativeCriterion provides existence/uniqueness
  criteria and IsProper.eq_valuativeCriterion / IsProper.of_valuativeCriterion.
  Actual properness of the relevant projective ambient family still needs
  an application/proof, rather than a bare reference to this interface.

HILBERT POLYNOMIALS, CYCLES, AND INTERSECTION NUMBERS

* RingTheory/Polynomial/HilbertPoly defines Polynomial.hilbertPoly p d
  from the coefficients of the rational power series p/(1-X)^d. It proves
  eventual polynomiality and uniqueness for that generating series.
  Its documentation explicitly lists Hilbert polynomials of finitely
  generated graded modules over Noetherian rings as TODO.
* Graded algebras/modules, homogeneous ideals, finite type, Noetherian
  graded algebras, and homogeneous localization are present. There is no
  located bridge from those to projective-family Hilbert polynomials,
  flat-family Hilbert polynomial constancy, or multigraded degree
  conservation.
* RingTheory/Length and FiniteLength provide actual module length,
  finiteness/positivity, and additivity in exact sequences. Associated
  primes, minimal primes, ideal height, and Krull dimension are present.
  These are useful local ingredients for defining cycle multiplicities.
* No library of algebraic cycles, fundamental cycles, Chow groups/rings,
  proper cycle pushforward, flat cycle pullback, intersection with
  hyperplane classes, effective cycle specialization, or conservation of
  multidegrees was found in this pinned version.
* RingTheory/Kaehler has the conormal MODULE I/I^2 and cotangent theory.
  That is not a ready construction of the paper's projective conormal
  variety or multiplicity-one Gauss-graph cycle.

CONSEQUENCES FOR AN HONEST ORIGINAL-ROUTE FORMALIZATION

A faithful assembly must separately construct the actual projective
Gauss-graph/conormal closed objects, the horizontal closure family over
the curve/DVR, and their relevant cycles or an equivalent actual
multigraded coordinate-ring invariant. It must then establish flatness,
properness, the target component inclusion with positive multiplicity,
and conservation of the relevant multidegree. Identifying generic
transverse slices with that invariant and computing the Fermat value are
additional geometric bridges.

Proving arithmetic for finitely supported formal sums with arbitrary
nonnegative weights does prove useful abstract cycle algebra. It does
not instantiate fundamental cycles of schemes or establish that the
chosen weights are actual multidegrees. Likewise, accepting a record
whose fields already assert flatness, conservation, or target inclusion
keeps those fields as explicit hypotheses; it does not prove them for
the paper's determinant degeneration. Any shortcut must expose this
boundary and connect its statement to the actual geometric objects.
