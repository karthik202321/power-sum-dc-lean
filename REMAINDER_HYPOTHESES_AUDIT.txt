REMAINING HYPOTHESES AUDIT -- updated 2026-10-10

This audit reviews the theorem types and the dependencies of the new exact
and border assemblies. It supersedes the former list which left generic
field incidence and border degree comparison open. Build and axiom-audit
results are recorded separately; this document does not certify a particular
compilation run merely because a source statement has been written.

1. EXACT THEOREMS: GEOMETRIC INPUTS DISCHARGED

ConormalExactMultiplicity.ordinary_exact_count and symmetric_exact_count
assume n>=3 and literal ExactRepresentation / SymmetricExactRepresentation
of the complex Fermat polynomial. The corresponding quadratic theorems have
the same assumptions. There is no arbitrary degree, section realization,
isolatedness, length, genericity, Bezout or count premise in these endpoints.

The general smooth-degree theorems assume exactly nonzero positive-degree
homogeneity, projective smoothness, n>=2, and actual determinant representation.
The proof constructs flags, a finite section, all its points and full lengths,
then derives the local-ring comparison and Bezout applicability.

Intermediate Section and local-ring-equivalence records contain substantive
facts. This is harmless only because final proofs construct them. In particular:
- ConormalGaussSection.exists_section constructs the record and its degree.
- The ordinary/symmetric LiftFamily constructors supply actual kernels,
  normalization and the common ordinary reduction.
- The local equivalences are proved from literal chart/graph ideal identities.
- Artinianity follows from the finite section algebra.
- Singular Bezout is proved, and its multidegree/distinctness hypotheses are
  verified for those actual systems.

The old GaussDegreeTheory is not constructed in full generality. Its old
conditional theorems remain, but are no longer the final exact endpoints.
The proof replaces a generic transverse point count by full section lengths;
it verifies the conormal/Gauss incidence route without needing reduced sections.
It is not claimed to reproduce every manuscript argument verbatim.

2. BEZOUT STATEMENT: NO EXCESS OR MULTIPLICITY SHORTCUT

SingularBezoutWithExcess.universal_multiplicity_bezout concerns a square
multihomogeneous system on three coordinate blocks and any finite family of
projectively distinct zeros. Each point has a valid chart and the actual
localized equation quotient is Artinian. The conclusion bounds the sum of
those actual module lengths by the multidegree class coefficient.
The selected zeros may be singular/nonreduced. Other components are unrestricted.
The two-factor native symmetric theorem is proved by an actual local-ring
isomorphism eliminating the dummy factor, not by equating unexplained weights.

The actual complete-intersection and local deformation conservation steps
are proved. No geometric count, global finiteness or all-chart simplicity
hypothesis is smuggled into this theorem. SINGULAR_BEZOUT_AUDIT.txt records
its dedicated review and concrete chart-local-ring scope.

3. BORDER CLOSURE AND REPRESENTATION FAMILY: CONSTRUCTED

Finite-support Euclidean border membership implies canonical Zariski border
by a proved continuous coefficient map and Euclidean-closure inclusion.
The actual universal determinant support is proved sufficient.
No equality of arbitrary Euclidean and Zariski closures is assumed.

Starting from a nonexact boundary point, the proof constructs an actual curve,
normalization, finite generic parameter extension, local DVR and residue map.
The generic determinant represents the actual coefficient polynomial and its
specialization is exactly Fermat. The generic representation field is proved
to be the fraction field of the DVR. Symmetry is retained where appropriate.
A common pole bound is proved. This is more than a supplied FamilyCertificate.

The actual horizontal Gauss family is flat, its target conormal containment
is proved, its defining ideals are proved bihomogeneous, and its actual
bigraded dimensions and Hilbert polynomial are conserved. Its special fibre
is not radicalized. These conclusions apply to the constructed family.

4. BORDER DEGREE: CONSTRUCTED LOCAL GAUSS-SECTION SPECIALIZATION

The new endpoint namespace is ConormalBorderMultiplicity. Its written final
statements have only these hypotheses:

- ordinary_zariski_count: n >= 3 and OrdinaryZariskiBorder n m.
- symmetric_zariski_count: n >= 3 and SymmetricZariskiBorder n m.
- ordinary_border_count / symmetric_border_count: n >= 3 and the respective
  OrdinaryFiniteSupportBorder / SymmetricFiniteSupportBorder membership.
- ordinary_border_quadratic / symmetric_border_quadratic: the same bare
  finite-support border membership and n >= 3.
- ordinary_borderAtSize_count / symmetric_borderAtSize_count and their
  quadratic versions use the canonical complete finite coefficient space.

The count conclusions are

  n*(n-1)^(n-2) <= ordinaryBezout m n,
  n*(n-1)^(n-2) <= symmetricBezout m n.

The quadratic conclusions are respectively

  (n-1)^2 / (4*exp(1)) <= m,
  (n-1)^2 / (2*exp(1)) <= m.

There is no geometric, finite-section, genericity, multiplicity-preservation,
closure-comparison, Bezout, field-embedding, cycle or count-budget parameter
in these endpoints. The following chain discharges the applicability facts.

(a) ACTUAL SPECIAL SECTION

ConormalGaussSection.exists_section constructs the special Fermat Gauss
section and its complete finite equation algebra. Its degree is the full
sum of local lengths n*(n-1)^(n-2), including nonreduced section points.
Smoothness, homogeneity, nonzeroness and the dimension restrictions are
proved for the actual Fermat polynomial in the endpoint proof.

(b) ACTUAL GENERIC SECTION, WITH EXACT SPECIAL RESIDUES

DeterminantDvrHomogeneous constructs a homogeneous lift of the actual DVR
coefficient polynomial. Generic and special dehomogenizations are exactly
the determinant and Fermat polynomials, and partial derivatives dehomogenize
to the corresponding actual affine derivatives. The generic polynomial is
nonzero because its specialization is nonzero; the hypersurface Hilbert
polynomial and degree bound <= n-1 are proved for that actual polynomial.

HomogeneousHilbertDegree proves degree nonincrease under homogeneous ideal
enlargement and degree drop under actual regular homogeneous cuts. The proof
of degree nonincrease uses a finite disjoint standard-monomial orthant
decomposition, so no positivity assumption is hidden in a Hilbert comparison.
SaturatedSectionHilbert iterates these facts and derives finite chart algebras.

PrescribedResidueGaussSequence chooses all generic derivative flags over the
DVR while retaining their prescribed special coefficients. It also constructs
a normalized primal flag with exact x0 coefficient -1. The regularity of these
flags on the actual successive saturated quotients is a conclusion.
FinitePrescribedGaussSection combines this construction with the proved
Hilbert-degree bound. Finiteness is a conclusion, not a supplied certificate.

SaturatedChartSmoothness proves that a homogeneous regular element stays a
non-zero-divisor on the actual chart l=1. In the finite chart it is a unit.
The construction therefore produces a scalar derivative combination which is
a unit on the whole finite scheme. Its support is consequently smooth over
every field extension. No reducedness or all-simple-points assumption enters.

ActualGenericGaussSection.exists_actual_section applies all of these results
to the actual determinant DVR family and dehomogenizes the quotient. It
constructs ell, a, an actual finite affine ideal J and a unit derivative
combination. The proved relation is stronger than a set-theoretic support
comparison:

  J = saturation(actual affine polar equation ideal, actual derivatives).

The constructor assumes the actual family and the already constructed special
section. Its final use constructs both. It has no unfulfilled family-specific
finiteness, smoothness, genericity, degree or preservation hypothesis.

(c) SPECIAL LOCAL LENGTHS ARE BOUNDED BY GENERIC LENGTH

DVRSaturatedGaussPersistence.family_saturated_contraction_preserves applies
local complete-intersection/parameter-regularity results to the actual lifted
polar and primal equations. At each selected special point a nonzero actual
derivative is a unit in its local ring. Derivative saturation and horizontal
contraction preserve the local special equation ideal there; nilpotents and
full local lengths are retained.

DVRSectionLength.selected_lengths_le_generic combines those proved local
annihilation facts with actual finite-algebra local decomposition, CRT and
the DVR fibre-dimension inequality. DVRGaussLengthBound.family_section_degree_le
applies it to the constructed family and selected Fermat section. The output
is its full special length <= finrank of the actual finite generic J quotient.
In the final assembly, the required finite J and saturation relations come
from ActualGenericGaussSection, rather than remaining assumptions about an
unspecified family.

(d) INCIDENCE BOUND OVER THE ACTUAL GENERIC FIELD

ArbitraryFieldFinitePolarBound proves ordinary and symmetric bounds for finite
saturated polar quotients over every characteristic-zero field. The proof
passes to its actual algebraic closure and proves scalar extension preserves
the complete quotient dimension. It does not pretend that the generic
function field embeds as a complex algebra into C.

The transported unit derivative combination proves every geometric support
point is smooth. The literal saturation sandwich identifies its actual local
equation ideal with the original polar equations. The incidence construction,
normalization, local-ring equivalences and isolated-zero Bezout hypotheses are
then proved for those points. The sum includes all local lengths, rather than
just the number of reduced support points.

(e) FINAL ASSEMBLY FROM BARE BORDER MEMBERSHIP

ordinary_family_count and symmetric_family_count construct the special
section, construct the finite generic section with matching special residues,
apply the lower length comparison, and apply the generic-field incidence
upper bound. Their family arguments are discharged by the proved
ordinary_border_exact_or_family / symmetric_border_exact_or_family split.
The exact branch uses the already proved exact theorem. The boundary branch
uses the actual constructed DVR determinant family.

Finite-support Euclidean border membership is sent to Zariski border by the
proved one-way inclusion needed here. No reverse polynomial-image closure
theorem or universal equality of Euclidean and Zariski closure is assumed.
The existing numerical estimates then give the two quadratic constants.

5. WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED ABOUT THE GLOBAL CYCLE FORMALIZATION

The numerical border route is a local Gauss-section specialization argument.
It does not complete a standalone library of glued projective fundamental
cycles, global purity/component identification, or a separately defined
intersection pairing on arbitrary products of projective spaces.

MixedHilbertComparison.top_mixed_coefficient_mono now proves the true top
mixed-Hilbert-coefficient comparison for literal nested bihomogeneous ideals,
using positive disjoint orthants. Its general total-degree hypothesis must
still be proved in any application. It does not identify those coefficients
with independent global intersection numbers merely by naming them degrees.
The new numerical border proof does not need that additional global package.

Legacy GaussDegreeTheory, FamilyCycleData, FamilyCertificate and the old
UniversalPolynomialImageClosure interface remain explicit conditional
interfaces in older modules. They are not claimed to be constructed in their
full generality. They do not occur as assumptions in the new exact or border
endpoint theorem statements. Their presence elsewhere must not be confused
with unresolved parameters of those new endpoints.

6. PROVENANCE AND CLAIM LIMIT

The development constructs general Gauss sections, varies their flags while
preserving special residues, and specializes their actual local lengths. The
coordinate x0=1 is the dehomogenizing coordinate of a newly constructed
homogeneous lift. It is not a fixed Fermat-coordinate slice used to count
explicit solutions. The dependency/source audit checks exclusion of the
alternate explicit-slice modules; this is not a proof of historical influence
or ownership of classical algebraic methods.

The correct verification claim is tied to the actual compiled theorem types:
when the integrated build and foundational-axiom audit pass, the new exact
and border numerical endpoints require only their displayed representation
or border-membership hypotheses. A clean axiom audit alone would not have
established that fact; the applicability constructions above are essential.
