DVR LOCAL PERSISTENCE — SCOPE AND HYPOTHESIS AUDIT

Module: Sheshadri.DVRRootPersistence

Theorem family_contraction_annihilates_local:
- D is a discrete valuation ring; L is its actual fraction field.
- epsilon : D -> k is a surjective ring map to an infinite field.
- pi is irreducible and ker epsilon is the principal ideal (pi).
- fs is a literal list of polynomials in D[x_1,...,x_N], with N entries.
- p is an actual common zero of the coefficient-specialized equations.
- The localization of k[x]/(special fs) at the actual point p is Artinian.

Conclusion: every equation in the contraction of the generic equation ideal
specializes to zero in the ENTIRE original local intersection algebra at p.
This is stronger than vanishing at the residue-field point.

Theorem specialized_contractedIdeal_localization_eq:
The coefficient-specialized generic contraction and the original special
ideal become EQUAL ideals in k[x] localized at p. Nilpotent structure, hence
every local multiplicity encoded by this quotient, is retained exactly.

Constructed proof ingredients:
1. The localized special fibre is explicitly isomorphic to k[x] localized at p.
2. Isolated square systems are regular sequences, via the already proved
   isolated complete-intersection theorem (no Jacobian assumption).
3. Local regular-sequence permutation makes the actual uniformizer regular
   after imposing the deformed equations.
4. Every nonzero DVR scalar is a unit times a power of the uniformizer.
   Therefore every generic denominator is cancellable in this quotient.
5. Localization/quotient maps identify the specialized original local algebra
   and prove equality of localized ideals.

No flatness, conservation-of-multiplicity, finite-neighborhood, generic-section,
transversality, count-budget, or projective-cycle premise occurs.

LIMITATION: this theorem alone does not prove a numerical comparison with the
generic fibre's total length. Such a comparison still requires a finite
neighborhood, finite-dimensional generic quotient, or a proved interpolation/
Hilbert-piece comparison. The theorem does not claim that arbitrary affine
families preserve total lengths: points can escape to infinity.

No fixed Fermat coordinate slice appears. The statement is general algebra
for actual square systems over arbitrary DVRs.

ADDITIONAL SATURATION INTERFACE
Module: Sheshadri.DVRSaturatedPersistence
The generic ideal J may be any ideal between the extended literal square
system and its saturation by a displayed family g_i. It suffices that one
specialized g_i is nonzero at the selected point. The localized specialized
contraction of J is then exactly the original local ideal. This allows
removing generic components on the gradient/boundary locus without deleting
any part of the chosen special local intersection algebra.

COMPLETION INFRASTRUCTURE (independent auxiliary route)
Modules: Sheshadri.AdicScalarCompatibility,
         Sheshadri.CompletionFiniteGeneration,
         Sheshadri.DVRCompletionDomain.
- Module and algebra presentations of principal-adic completion are explicitly
  linearly equivalent over the base and agree on canonical images.
- Injectivity of multiplication by the actual parameter survives Noetherian
  completion, in either presentation.
- Generation modulo an ideal implies generation after completion; a basis
  modulo a regular parameter yields a genuine completed linear equivalence.
- The actual inverse-limit completion of a DVR is proved to be a domain by
  finite-quotient valuation estimates, so its fraction field is legitimate.
These lemmas alone do NOT finish the border proof through completion. In
particular relative flatness over the tensor product with the completed base
is not asserted or inferred merely from flatness over the original ring.

APPLIED ACTUAL-FAMILY BRIDGE
Module: Sheshadri.DVRSaturatedGaussPersistence
family_saturated_contraction_preserves accepts the actual constructed
DeterminantDvrFamily.Family, an actual constructed Section, flags with their
specified residues, and a generic ideal J lying between the actual generic
polar equations and their actual derivative saturation. It derives the DVR
and fraction-field instances from Family. It proves:
(1) the original special Gauss ideal is contained in the special contraction;
(2) every contracted equation annihilates every selected full local algebra.
The nonvanishing denominator is obtained from Section.smooth at each point.
section_saturated_contraction_local_eq proves the actual local ideal equality.
The generic ideal sandwich remains an intermediate construction condition;
this module does not itself assert the existence/finiteness of such a J.
