MATHEMATICAL CLARIFICATIONS IN THE REVISED PAPER

The manuscript's conservation argument now uses constancy of the Euler
characteristic of every bidegree twist in the proper flat family. Equality
of the two-variable numerical polynomials identifies all multiplicity-weighted
multidegrees. It does not infer rational equivalence merely because the base
is an arbitrary smooth curve, and it does not use a single Segre Hilbert
polynomial to recover all mixed coefficients. The roadmap, glossary,
standard-inputs table and explanatory multidegree passage are aligned with
this correction. Precise Stacks Project references are added.

The cone comparison now restricts radial projection to the complement of the
vertex. A primal hyperplane avoiding the vertex gives an actual isomorphism
with the base hypersurface, and its conormal section preserves the dual
coordinate with multiplicity one. No global projective-line-bundle map through
the vertex is asserted.

These are repairs to the human-readable global exposition. The Lean endpoints
use actual local Gauss sections and full local-ring lengths. The release does
not claim verbatim formalization of every global-cycle assertion in the paper,
nor a complete independent Chow/intersection library.

VERIFIED ENDPOINTS AND REPRODUCTION

For n >= 3, literal ordinary exact or border representations imply
  (n-1)^2/(4e) <= m.
The corresponding symmetric endpoints imply
  (n-1)^2/(2e) <= m.
Finite-support Euclidean, canonical-support Euclidean, and literal Zariski
border predicates are exposed. Final statements require no geometric
certificate, assumed Bezout theorem, genericity, finite-section or
specialization premise. Stronger coefficient-count bounds remain available.

The archive contains the complete local Lean entry-point source graph.
It is not an offline distribution of Lean, Mathlib or their compiled caches.
Initial dependency downloads are required; exact toolchain/dependency pins,
build commands and paper compilation instructions are supplied in README.md,
paper/BUILDING.txt and the verification appendix. The delivered verification
