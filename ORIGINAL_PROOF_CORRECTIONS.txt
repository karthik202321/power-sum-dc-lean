THREE LOCAL REPAIRS PRESERVING THE ORIGINAL PROOF ROUTE

These are mathematical corrections/clarifications to the supplied revised

1. SECTION 4.3: REDUCED RIGHT EQUATIONS

Replace the standalone assertion that full and reduced right-kernel ideals
agree by the assertion that they agree after adjoining the full left-kernel
ideal. In a Schur chart set
  a = u'^T + r B^(-1),   z = v' + B^(-1)c,
  g = s - r B^(-1)c.
The left ideal is (a,g). Reduced right equations have form M_v*z + lambda*g
with M_v invertible. Consequently
  I_left + I_reduced_right = (a,g,z) = I_left + I_full_right.
The combined local ideal equality actually used for isolatedness survives.
ConormalIncidence also proves the corresponding full tangent implication.

2. LEMMA 7.4: MULTIDEGREE CONSERVATION

A family over an arbitrary smooth curve yields algebraic equivalence, not
general rational equivalence of fibre cycles. The fixed biprojective ambient
allows the desired Chow-class conclusion, but it needs a justification.
Use the following direct conservation argument instead.

Let d=n-1. For each pair of integers a,b, O_W(a,b) is flat over C. Properness
and flatness make chi(W_c,O_W_c(a,b)) independent of c on the connected base.
The degree-d homogeneous part of its two-variable numerical polynomial is
  sum_(k=0)^d delta_k([W_c]) * a^(d-k)*b^k / ((d-k)!*k!).
Therefore all delta_k are independent of c. These coefficients incorporate
the generic-length multiplicities of the scheme-theoretic fibre. Lemma 7.3
then identifies the generic cycle with Gamma_c.

One single Segre Hilbert polynomial would not determine every multidegree.
The two-variable polynomial, or enough distinct polarizations, does. The
formal numerical bridge uses chi(W_c,O(q,q*t)), first identifies the full
polynomial on the natural q,t grid, then extracts its coefficient of q^d.

If rational equivalence in the ambient is desired afterward, use the Chow
ring of P^n x (P^n)^dual: its cycle classes are determined by these multidegrees.
The inequality only needs numerical conservation.

3. PROPOSITION 9.1: CONE SHIFT AWAY FROM THE VERTEX

Do not assert a global P^1-bundle morphism Con(X_Z) -> Con(Z).
Distinct tangencies sharing a tangent hyperplane can meet in the same
vertex-covector pair, preventing a global projection of that form.

Restrict radial projection to primal points away from the cone vertex q_0.
Choose the generic primal hyperplane M with q_0 not in M. Each ruling meets
M once, and X_Z intersect M -> Z is an isomorphism. On this section, the
conormal graph is identified with Con(Z), preserving the dual coordinate.
Thus the required intersection gives
  delta_(n-2)(Con(X_Z)) = degree(Z^dual).
For a smooth degree-e hypersurface Z in P^(n-1), in characteristic zero,
the conormal projection onto its hypersurface dual is birational, and
  degree(Z^dual) = e*(e-1)^(n-2).

The Lean file ConormalCone proves the representative-section identities
and scaling compatibility. The projective conormal/intersection-degree
bridge and dual-degree theorem remain external geometric inputs.

Primary reference:
  E. Tevelev, Projectively Dual Varieties, Theorem 1.10(b), printed p.6;
  Example 7.4, printed pp.106-107:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0112028

CHECK ON BEZOUT

The remaining UniversalSimpleBezout assumption concerns specified distinct
simple isolated zeros of a square multiprojective system. It does not require
all zeros to be isolated, so excess positive-dimensional components are
allowed. This is a restricted consequence of the isolated-zero theorem:
  J.-P. Dedieu, G. Malajovich, M. Shub, On the Curvature of the Central Path
  of Linear Programming Theory, Section 5, Theorem 5.1:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0312083
Its truth was source-audited earlier; the theorem itself remains unproved
in Lean. The new original route does not use the polynomial-image
Euclidean/Zariski closure assumption from the earlier alternative proof.
