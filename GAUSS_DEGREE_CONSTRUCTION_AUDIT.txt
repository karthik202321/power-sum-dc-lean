GAUSS DEGREE / ORIGINAL CONORMAL ROUTE — CONSTRUCTION AUDIT

Scope of the existing interface

The legacy ConormalExact.GaussDegreeTheory remains an interface, not an
intrinsic construction of a conormal multidegree. The new unconditional
ConormalExactMultiplicity exact theorems do not consume this interface. Its arbitrary-polynomial section
field is not visibly inconsistent: degree zero and an empty root family
can handle zero polynomials, repeated factors, or other cases without
smooth roots. But outside the smooth-homogeneous law it may select a
realizable cardinal rather than the degree of a canonical scheme.
Axiom auditing alone therefore cannot validate its geometric meaning.

The transverse-section field is stronger infrastructure than the exact
bound needs now that isolated local scheme lengths satisfy the proved
singular excess-compatible Bezout theorem. The following construction uses
actual generic Gauss polars and preserves their nonreduced local algebras.
It does not assume generic transversality or identify length with a reduced
point count.

Proved geometric/algebraic inputs

SmoothGaussPolars proves Euler's polynomial identity for any homogeneous
form in characteristic zero. For positive degree, f belongs to its literal
gradient ideal. Consequently smoothness away from the origin on f=0
implies that the derivative system has no common zero off the origin.
Nullstellensatz then proves every coordinate is in the radical of that
actual gradient ideal. The derivative list and its span/ideal are linked
exactly to actual linear combinations of partial derivatives.

Its gaussFlag_ideal_eq_polar_ideal is an equality of ideals, including all
nilpotents: unrestricted projective Gauss flags differ from ordinary polar
linear combinations only by an explicit scalar multiple of f. Thus the
polar section is the actual conormal/Gauss flag section in the cone chart.
This is not a replacement by a target-specific coordinate slice.

GenericGaussPolars constructs the coefficients of a regular polar on every
actual irrelevant-saturated quotient. The input is the literal smoothness
condition and not a basepoint-free, count, or genericity certificate. The
finite associated-prime avoidance used here is general algebraic linear
system selection.

IrrelevantSaturation constructs the ideal consisting of polynomials q for
which, for each coordinate Xi, some power Xi^N*q lies in I. This is the
intersection of coordinate-open contractions. The source proves inclusion,
idempotence, the irrelevant-saturated property, homogeneous closure, and
that a fixed power of the entire irrelevant ideal annihilates sat(I)/I.
SaturationHilbertTail independently turns this into equality of all large
actual homogeneous pieces. Hence low-degree irrelevant torsion is not
silently discarded in a claimed global Hilbert-series equality.

GenericGaussPolarSequence constructs actual coefficients ell_j at every
step. The next derivative polar is regular on the actual saturation and
therefore filter regular on the preceding literal equation ideal. Neither
irrelevant saturation nor a regular-sequence condition is an unproved
input for these constructed polars.

Constructed Hilbert degree and its meaning

For a nonzero smooth homogeneous f of degree d in n variables, SmoothGaussDegree now constructs an actual sequence of n-2 derivative polars such
that the sufficiently large graded pieces of

  K[z_1,...,z_n] / (f, polar_1, ..., polar_(n-2))

have dimension exactly d*(d-1)^(n-2). The full locked build passed, and its
principal theorem uses only propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound.
FilterRegularCompleteIntersection
computes this by actual eventual Hilbert-polynomial differences. This is
the length degree of the Gauss-polar section. It is not by itself a theorem
that the dual hypersurface is birationally parametrized by the Gauss map.
The latter reflexivity assertion in paper Lemma 3.7 is separate and is
unnecessary for the stated conormal-pullback degree computation.

SmoothGaussSection constructs the actual coefficients of a further
filter-regular linear form L and proves that every Xi
belong to radical(I+(L)). The proof uses the vanishing of the finite
difference of an eventually constant Hilbert function. Consequently L
avoids the entire projective support, not merely some selected points.
The ordinary chart quotient is the literal ring K[z]/(I,L-1).
OrdinaryProjectiveHilbert proves actual high-piece/chart bijectivity,
finite-dimensionality, and equality of its vector-space dimension with
the sum of all local module lengths, using ArtinianLocalDecomposition.
SmoothGaussSectionLength.exists_polar_section_length integrates these
results: both flags are constructed, the actual chart quotient is finite,
and the full local-length sum is exactly d*(d-1)^(n-2). Its locked build
passed 2331/2331. The source also proves every literal chart point is
nonzero and smooth. Thus this specific section-length realization is now
a theorem, rather than an input resembling GaussDegreeTheory.

On the cone, choosing the primal hyperplane x0-L(z)=0 gives x0=1 and
L(z)=1 on this actual chart. It avoids the cone vertex. The inherited
Gauss flag conditions are the polars modulo f by the proved ideal identity.
The determinant incidence comparison is now constructed at the level of
actual local rings. ConormalPolarIdeal proves the derivative/trace flag
identity and identifies the determinant-polar ideal with the literal
chosen Gauss section ideal. OrdinaryIncidenceLocalMultiplicity and
SymmetricIncidenceLocalMultiplicity identify the literal projective
incidence-chart local rings with those actual polar local rings, including
all nilpotents. The ordinary reduction matrix is chosen simultaneously
for the finite actual point family using the proved generic reduction
lemma; its nonzero augmented determinant is proved, not assumed.

ConormalExactMultiplicity constructs the finite section, its normalized
kernel lifts, the common reduction, the actual incidence charts, their
Artinian local rings, and preservation of their full lengths. The proved
singular excess-compatible Bezout theorem therefore bounds the sum, and
the constructed Gauss-polar section supplies the exact lower quantity

  d*(d-1)^(n-2).

The main smooth homogeneous endpoints require only a literal determinant
representation, positive degree, a nonzero homogeneous polynomial, and
its literal smoothness away from the origin. The symmetric endpoint also
requires literal symmetry of the coefficient matrices. Their Fermat
specializations require only n>=3 and the ordinary/symmetric literal
representation. Thus both exact coefficient bounds and constants
1/(4e), 1/(2e) now have an unconditional conormal/Gauss-polar proof. They
have no GaussDegreeTheory argument, no assumed Bezout proposition, no
section-length certificate, and no transversality/reducedness hypothesis.
The complete final source passed a direct Lean check; its serialized build
and principal theorem axiom audit are recorded in the final status below.

Checks against misleading claims

* No assumption that an arbitrary excess system is globally zero-dimensional
  is used or should be reintroduced at the determinant incidence stage.
* No assertion that generic Cox equations form a global regular sequence
  is used. Only the actually proved filter-regular statements enter the
  Hilbert-polynomial computation.
* No global P1-bundle projection through the cone vertex is claimed. The
  actual hyperplane chart avoids that vertex. The paper's global fibre
  wording requires this qualification.
* Selecting a generic projective chart after constructing the polar section
  is not a special-coordinate Fermat counting method. No assertion of
  historical originality or independence of mathematical ideas follows
  from these formal proofs or this source audit.
* The normalized-curve conormal specialization/cycle-comparison obligations
  for the border theorem remain separate from this smooth special-side
  degree calculation. These files do not construct the whole border
  FamilyCertificate.

hidden geometric premise in its bihomogeneity or Hilbert constancy route.

by actual fraction fields of every prime quotient of the target algebra.
This correctly permits nonreduced/empty charts. Independent primal and

constructed horizontal ideal. Actual field fibre dimensions agree in
