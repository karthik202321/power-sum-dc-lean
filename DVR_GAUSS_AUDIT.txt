DVR GAUSS FAMILY AUDIT

Scope: original conormal specialization, with actual algebraic DVR families.
This audit describes proved statements, not a claim that the final border
numerical inequality has already been assembled.

1. Actual generic and horizontal Gauss ideals

DVRGaussContainment constructs the source equation F(z)=0 with independent
nonzero scalars mu, nu. On each derivative-open chart, the literal ambient
coordinates are
  primal = mu * (1,z)
  dual   = nu * (-sum_j z_j partial_j F, partial_j F).
The chart denominator is mu*nu*partial_j F. The generic chart also inverts
every nonzero scalar of the DVR. genericGaussIdeal is the intersection of
the radicals of the actual fraction-field graph-map kernels.

The generic support is proved equal to the Zariski closure of the union of
these genuine chart images on prime spectra. horizontalGaussIdeal is proved
to be the contraction of that generic ideal, and its prime-spectrum support
is the closure of the generic support. The horizontal quotient is flat over
the DVR. No arbitrary closure, flatness, graph-realization, or curve-family
certificate occurs in these conclusions.

2. Actual special conormal containment

If the special polynomial is nonzero, multiplication by a uniformizer is
proved injective on the hypersurface quotient. The proof uses the literal
residue polynomial and cancellation; DVR factorization then gives scalar
torsion-freeness. This survives localization to every derivative-open graph
chart, so each such chart injects into its generic base localization.

Consequently every smooth special Gauss point annihilates the horizontal
ideal. Taking the original ConormalZariski.conormalClosure proves containment
in the actual special fibre. specialFibreIdeal is coefficient extension of
the horizontal ideal, WITHOUT a radical after specialization. Thus this is
support containment in a scheme-theoretic fibre that may be nonreduced.
It does not assert equality of that fibre with the target conormal.

3. Bihomogeneity is proved

DVRGaussHomogeneous classifies every field-valued point of each generic chart
as a literal smooth scaled Gauss point. Conversely, every such smooth point
over a fraction-field extension evaluates the actual generic chart.
Independent rescaling of mu and nu preserves this description.

HomogeneousVanishing extracts each two-block homogeneous component from
vanishing under all nonzero rescalings. RadicalKernelFieldPoints tests in
ALL fraction fields of prime quotients of the generic chart. This use of all
prime quotients detects radical-kernel membership even if the chart algebra
is not reduced. It is not a test only at complex closed points and does not
assume any fraction-field embedding into the residue field.

The resulting theorems prove actual genericGaussIdeal_bihomogeneous,
horizontalGaussIdeal_bihomogeneous, and specialFibreIdeal_bihomogeneous.
An infinite generic field is used for polynomial coefficient extraction;
the constructed determinant families have a complex field algebra, which
supplies this hypothesis.

4. Actual Hilbert conservation

specialFibre_bicoordinate_dimensions proves equality of the vector-space
dimensions of the literal special and generic quotient pieces at EVERY
bidegree. This follows from the proved bihomogeneity, horizontal contraction,
and scalar torsion-freeness over the DVR. Special-fibre nilpotents remain
part of those quotient dimensions.

exists_common_gaussHilbertPolynomial constructs a common eventual bivariate
Hilbert polynomial and proves both evaluation laws. No proposed Hilbert
polynomial, mixed-degree formula, or coefficient comparison is assumed.

5. Bare border predicates discharge the family inputs

DeterminantDvrGaussContainment uses the actual Family produced by
DeterminantDvrFamily. This record includes a normalized DVR with a complex
residue map, uniformizer, genuine fraction field containing the generic
matrix-pencil parameters, and literal residue/generic polynomial identities.
The nonzero special-polynomial input is proved from the Fermat polynomial.

family_fermatConormal_subset and family_horizontalGauss_flat require only
that actual algebraic Family (and n>0 for the nonzero Fermat polynomial).
family_specialFibre_bicoordinate_dimensions applies Hilbert conservation to
the actual residue field C and actual generic representation field L.

ordinary_border_exact_or_conormalFamily and its symmetric counterpart start
from canonical Zariski border membership. The finiteSupportBorder versions
start from the paper-facing literal Euclidean finite-support border predicate:
BorderDvrFamily proves its inclusion in canonical Zariski closure, without
assuming equality of Euclidean and Zariski closures. The result is either an
exact complex representation or a constructed DVR family with the proved
special-conormal containment. The symmetric endpoint retains the literal
symmetry of every generic matrix-pencil coefficient.

6. Exact remaining scope restrictions

These results do not identify all positive-dimensional projective cycle
classes or prove effective comparison of the required mixed top-degree
coefficient between the target conormal and the special fibre. Such a step
still needs actual dimension/purity and effective-cycle or equivalent
sectional arguments, as appropriate to the final theorem.

Pointwise inequalities between bivariate Hilbert functions alone do NOT
imply inequalities of individual mixed coefficients. For example,
a^2-a*b+b^2 is positive for positive a,b but has a negative mixed coefficient.
No such inference is made by the new modules.

The generic determinant family may live over a representation fraction field
L larger than C. A final numerical border argument must apply the incidence
budget over the appropriate field or prove a justified scalar-extension or
closed-fibre reduction. These modules do not silently substitute C for L.

This construction follows the original Gauss/conormal specialization route.
The two scalars only change projective representatives. It does not impose a
fixed Fermat-coordinate slice or invoke a Kumar--Volk explicit-slice argument.

family/finite-support-border endpoints were separately axiom-audited and
