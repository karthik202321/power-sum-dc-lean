2026-10-09

Integration addendum: after this independent review, ActualGaussBaseChange
was added and compiled. It proves the affine-line tensor fibre ring equals
the specialized-ideal quotient, with the residue ring explicitly isomorphic
to C. The ring-identification portion mentioned below is therefore discharged;

Verdict
-------
The new modules discharge substantive geometric obligations, not merely
repackage all of them as certificates. They do NOT complete the original
exact or border theorem from its stated representation hypotheses. The
remaining gap is formal infrastructure connecting the constructed algebraic
objects to a genuine projective cycle/intersection theory over the selected
smooth algebraic curve.

The current conditional end theorems are honest about that gap. Compilation
and absence of custom axioms do not discharge their explicit arguments
UniversalSimpleBezout, GaussDegreeTheory, or FamilyCertificate.

This audit concerns the current source statements, definitions and proof
chain. It is not a claim to have developed an independent complete
intersection theory. No explicit Fermat polar slice has been introduced.

1. Newly constructed and proved objects
--------------------------------------

ActualGaussFamily constructs the literal smooth Gauss representative graph,
its polynomial Zariski closure, the radical vanishing ideal, and its reduced
coordinate quotient. It proves algebraic/Euclidean closedness and the special
fibre containment using smooth-root persistence. The specialized fibre ideal
is NOT radicalized, so potential multiplicities are retained.

The former arbitrary-total-family, graph-inclusion and algebraic-fibre
certificate fields have genuinely been removed. The family now used in
ActualConormalSpecialization is the concrete polynomial closure.

FermatIrreducible and FermatConormalIrreducible prove irreducibility of the
literal Fermat polynomial and of the actual rescaled Gauss closure at any
specialization equal to Fermat. The conormal argument uses an irreducible
open subset in a combined affine coordinate space and a literal polynomial
map. It does not incorrectly replace that algebraic topology by the product
of separate Zariski topologies. The target irreducibility certificate field
is now discharged for n >= 3.

ProjectiveConormal uses mathlib Projectivization for both coordinate blocks.
It proves independent-scaling invariance of algebraic closure, quotient
closedness, and the exact equality between projectivization of the affine
closure and closure of the projective affine Gauss graph. For power sums of
degree at least two, conormal_eq_homogeneousGaussClosure identifies this with
the closure of the literal homogeneous-gradient graph, including the points
at infinity. These are genuine projective points and an explicitly defined
quotient Zariski topology, not abstract conormal labels.

ConormalOffVertex propagates actual polynomial cone/dual/minor equations
through algebraic closure. Nonvanishing of the gradient then recovers the
actual Gauss covector off the primal vertex. Theorems
conormal_off_vertex_eq_gauss and conormal_hyperplaneSection_eq_gauss prove
that closing the graph adds no off-vertex points and that a primal
hyperplane avoiding the vertex cuts precisely the smooth Gauss graph.
ProjectiveConormal.projectiveHyperplaneSectionEquiv is a genuine projective
section equivalence. None of these theorems asserts an intersection number
or a global bundle projection across the vertex.

ActualGaussFlatness and ActualGaussFlatScheme prove saturation, absence of
base torsion and flatness of the ACTUAL total coordinate ring and spectrum
over C[t], for an OPEN good-parameter subset of the complex affine line.
The openness assumption is used in the persistence argument. This result
has real content and is not a flatness certificate.

ActualGaussCoefficientRing puts the base variable in the coefficient ring.
HomogeneousVanishing / ActualGaussHomogeneous /
ActualGaussCoefficientHomogeneous prove the required homogeneity of actual
vanishing ideals, including bihomogeneity over C[t]. FlatGradedHilbert proves
finite generation of each coordinate piece, its split projection when the
ideal is bihomogeneous, and constancy of finite-dimensional field base
changes of finite torsion-free modules over a PID.

ActualGaussHilbert proves constant tensor dimensions of the actual
coordinate modules over the affine line. Its eventual Hilbert-polynomial
and multidegree statements still explicitly assume existence of polynomials
representing those dimensions and the leading-coefficient identifications.
That distinction is essential.

AffineFundamentalCycle constructs the finite cycle of actual minimal primes
of an arbitrary Noetherian ring, weighted by actual generic local-ring
lengths. It proves positivity, whole-spectrum support and coefficient one
when the ring is reduced. ActualGaussCycles applies this to the actual total
and specialized fibre quotient rings and constructs actual closed fibre
points from complex representatives. Fibre multiplicities are retained.

AlgebraicCurveSelection proves polynomial-image coordinate-ring and
constructibility results and constructs Noether normalization. Its current
source explicitly stops short of cutting out and normalizing the required
curve through the specified boundary point.

2. Scope separations that must be preserved
------------------------------------------

AFFINE LINE IS NOT AN ARBITRARY CURVE.
The original proof selects and normalizes an algebraic curve. A polynomial
map from the affine line through every relevant boundary point has not been
proved and must not be assumed. Local DVR flatness along a normalized curve
is mathematically a different application from the implemented C[t] case.
The actual family may be defined over an embedded curve using the general
parameter construction, but its flatness/purity over that curve has not yet
been established in Lean.

PROJECTIVE POINTS/TOPOLOGY ARE NOT A PROJECTIVE SCHEME.
ProjectiveConormal does establish the set/topological projectivization
bridge. It does not construct relative biprojective Proj, a structure sheaf,
properness, local scheme rings, or the scheme-theoretic fibre cycle. Those
cannot be claimed merely because the projective points are now literal
mathlib Projectivization values.

AFFINE GENERIC LENGTHS ARE NOT YET PROJECTIVE CYCLE WEIGHTS.
The new cycle weights genuinely are local-ring lengths of actual affine
rings. The source does not yet glue them across projective charts, remove
irrelevant zero-block components, or identify their lengths with those of
the corresponding projective fibre scheme. Reducedness of the TOTAL ring
proves its own generic weights are one; it does NOT prove every special
fibre reduced or the generic fibre-cycle identification with Gamma_c.

FINITE MODULE DIMENSIONS ARE NOT YET MULTIDEGREES.
The constant tensor dimensions are proved. Homogeneous splitting is also
available as a proved theorem. Constructing the Hilbert polynomial, proving
its eventual equality with the actual fibre Hilbert function, and identifying
its top coefficients with intersection degrees remain necessary. In
particular a polynomial given as an argument together with these identities
is not a constructed Hilbert polynomial. Identifying the tensor fibre ring
with the specialized-ideal presentation and matching its graded pieces must
also be made explicit in a completed geometric assembly.

SUPPORT CONTAINMENT IS NOT YET POSITIVE COMPONENT MULTIPLICITY.
Target irreducibility and containment are now proved for the actual target.
To apply the finite-component comparison to the actual cycle, its component
supports must be identified and the dimension/purity premises discharged.
An irreducible closed subset can be properly contained in a larger
irreducible component; containment by itself is insufficient.

3. Exact remaining obligations of the end theorem
------------------------------------------------

A. Construct an inhabitant of GaussDegreeTheory from actual projective
   conormal geometry. This requires the generic transverse section theorem
   and the smooth homogeneous hypersurface degree formula. The current
   interface is still uninhabited by a proved construction. The projective
   cone section and off-vertex statements do not themselves prove the
   numerical formula d*(d-1)^(n-2).

B. Supply/prove the universal isolated-point multihomogeneous Bezout input.
   It remains a visible hypothesis, not a new custom axiom. Prior source
   audits support the statement's mathematical validity, but that is not a
   Lean proof of it.

C. Construct the required normalized smooth curve and good open subset from
   actual border membership, together with the coefficient family and the
   approach to its special point. Constructibility/Noether normalization are
   genuine progress toward this, not completion of it.

D. Construct the projective family over that curve, prove flatness/purity,
   identify the generic fibre cycle with multiplicity-one Gamma_c after a
   finite shrink, and connect the actual special affine/projective cycles to
   FamilyCycleData. This includes the actual dimension and strict-dimension
   drop facts used by the component comparison.

E. Complete actual Hilbert/intersection theory and its family conservation,
   and identify both target and generic degrees with the constructed
   GaussDegreeTheory. FamilyCertificate's remaining cycle, dimension, Euler
   and degree-identification fields are still real obligations.

4. Appropriate completion claim
-------------------------------

Safe: a significantly enlarged verification of the original conormal route,
with concrete family ideals/rings, actual affine local multiplicities, target
irreducibility, projective conormal identification, off-vertex cone-section
geometry and affine-line flat/Hilbert-module results proved without custom
axioms or the alternate explicit polar slice.

Unsafe: the original exact theorem is fully verified; the border theorem
follows from bare border membership; the arbitrary smooth-curve projective
family is constructed and flat; all cycle and intersection identifications
are completed; the remaining interfaces are mere bookkeeping.

No contradiction or false closed theorem was found in the newly audited
source statements. The principal remaining risk is overstating the scope of
those correct theorems when presenting the overall result.
